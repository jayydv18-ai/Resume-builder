Privacy Policy

At GoResume, protecting your privacy is a priority. Read how our free Resume Builder handles your data and safeguards your information.

Information we collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide a smooth resume-building experience. This may include details you enter into the builder, contact information for support requests, and anonymous usage data to improve our platform.

How we use your data

Your information helps us save your resume progress, respond to inquiries, and enhance our free Resume Builder over time. We do not share your personal resume content with third parties unless required by law.

Security and storage

We use industry-standard security measures to protect data and maintain a reliable resume tool. Our goal is to keep your information safe while delivering fast, dependable service.

Your choices

You can request updates or deletion of your personal details. If you have questions about our privacy practices, contact our support team at support@goresume.com.
